Understanding IB French

A quick overview

The International Baccalaureate (IB) offers the Diploma Programme (DP) in Grades 11–12. Pre-IB French helps younger students build the language and academic skills they need before entering the DP. In the Diploma Programme, the French course depends on the student’s prior language experience and school placement.

Grades 9–10

Pre-IB French

Improve paragraphs, comprehension, speaking and careful use of grammar.

Diploma · Beginner

French ab initio SL

Build everyday communication and foundational grammar. Available at Standard Level only.

Diploma · Previous French study

French B SL / HL

Explore IB themes, develop more sophisticated communication and practise course-specific assessment tasks. HL includes literary study.

IB-style French practice activities

Here are original classroom exercises that illustrate skills we practise in each track—not official IB examination questions.

Pre-IB · Reading & grammar

Complete the sentence

Hier, nous ______ au musée et nous ______ beaucoup de choses.

Choose the correct past-tense verbs and explain why.

ab initio SL · Writing

A message to a friend

Tu vas organiser une fête pour ton anniversaire. Écris un message à ton ami(e) : indique la date, le lieu et les activités prévues.

Practise everyday language, clear organization and the right register.

French B SL · Paper 1 style

Write for an audience

Ton école souhaite réduire les déchets plastiques. Rédige un texte destiné aux élèves pour proposer des changements et les convaincre de participer.

Choose an appropriate text type and develop convincing arguments.

French B HL · Literary discussion

Analyze an extract

Comment le narrateur révèle-t-il les tensions entre l'individu et la société dans cet extrait ? Justifie ta réponse avec deux éléments du texte.

Practise interpretation, evidence and structured oral analysis.

Exercises are adapted to the student’s current course and syllabus. These are teaching activities, not reproduced IB exam papers.
