About This Course

There is a stage between “I know some French” and “I am ready for intensive exam preparation.”

The TCF/TEF Pathway is designed for that stage. It is for learners who already have basic French but still need more range, accuracy, speed and confidence before focused exam preparation becomes truly useful.

The priority stays on language development. At the same time, you will gradually work with longer responses, more structured prompts, light time pressure and communication tasks that begin preparing you for the demands of TCF or TEF.

What You'll Build

Speaking

Give longer answers, explain experiences, compare options, justify ideas and support opinions.

Listening

Follow increasingly natural French and identify key information more efficiently.

Reading

Work with longer everyday, professional and opinion-based texts with improving speed and accuracy.

Writing

Organize ideas more clearly, connect sentences, describe situations and develop more complete responses.

How the Pathway Progresses

Step 1

Strengthen the language

Stabilize core grammar, sentence building and high-frequency vocabulary.

Step 2

Use it in real communication

Speak, listen, read and write through increasingly demanding communication tasks.

Step 3

Bridge into exam preparation

Begin working with more structured prompts, longer responses and light time constraints.

Prerequisites

This is an Elementary to Developing Intermediate program, generally suited to learners around CEFR A2 and moving toward B1.

You should consider this course if:

  • you've completed French Foundations, or have equivalent basic French
  • you can hold simple conversations but are not yet ready for intensive exam preparation
  • you understand many basic grammar concepts but still struggle to use them consistently in real communication
  • you can understand some spoken and written French but lose accuracy when the language becomes faster or more complex
You do not need to identify your own CEFR level perfectly. If you are unsure whether you belong in French Foundations, TCF/TEF Pathway or TCF/TEF Canada Preparation, contact us and we can help confirm the best starting point.

Who Should Attend

You may understand basic grammar and everyday vocabulary, but still hesitate when you speak, struggle to organize longer answers, miss too much in listening, or find that your French becomes less reliable when tasks become more demanding.

That is exactly the gap this course is designed to close.

This pathway may be right for you if...

  • you've already studied French and can manage basic everyday communication
  • you know grammar rules but can't always use them quickly and accurately when speaking or writing
  • you need more vocabulary, sentence-building range and confidence before intensive exam work
  • listening feels manageable when French is slow, but difficult at a more natural pace
  • you want to work toward TCF or TEF, but don't want every class to feel like a mock exam

This is probably not your course if...

  • you're completely new to French — start with French Foundations instead
  • you already communicate comfortably at a solid intermediate level and mainly need timing, strategy, simulations and exam-specific feedback
  • you're looking only for a last-minute crash course just before an exam date

The French Up Approach

Strong exam performance starts with stronger French.

Exam strategies matter. But they work best when the French underneath them is strong enough.

Our approach is to build that language first, while keeping the exam ahead in view.

We start where you are.

Foundations, Pathway and Preparation are designed for different stages. We help you start where the work will be most useful.

We work on the French behind the answer.

Feedback addresses grammar, vocabulary, clarity, organization and communication — not just whether you completed the task correctly.

We add difficulty as your control improves.

Longer answers, time limits and exam-style constraints are added as your control improves, so you can build accuracy before working under more pressure.

We build skills that carry over.

The goal isn't to perform one rehearsed answer well. It's to become more capable of handling unfamiliar questions and tasks in French.

What happens after the Pathway?

The next step depends on the French you have built.

The Pathway is designed to move you closer to exam readiness, but it does not promise the same outcome for every learner. Your starting point, consistency, outside practice and target result all matter.

Ready for exam-focused work

Move into TCF / TEF Canada Preparation

If your French is strong enough, the next stage shifts toward timing, strategy, task execution, simulations, corrections and exam performance.

Continue making progress

Keep strengthening targeted skills

If one or two areas still need work, we can recommend continued language development or targeted private coaching before intensive exam preparation.
