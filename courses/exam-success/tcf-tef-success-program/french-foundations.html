About This Course

French Foundations is designed for complete beginners and elementary learners who need to build a solid base before moving toward TCF or TEF-focused training. You'll develop the grammar, vocabulary, and four-skill basics needed for everyday communication.

This program is organized in three progressive internal stages — Foundations Level 1, 2 and 3 — but you won't need to choose one yourself. We assess your current French and place you in the right group, and you move through the stages as you're ready, with no separate enrollment or extra fee between them.

What You'll Learn

Speaking

Build confidence forming sentences, answering questions, and talking about everyday situations.

Listening

Understand common words, phrases, questions, and increasingly natural spoken French.

Reading

Understand short messages, everyday texts, and increasingly detailed written information.

Writing

Construct clear sentences and communicate basic information accurately in writing.

Prerequisites

This is a Beginner to Elementary program (CEFR A0–A2). You should enroll in this course if:

  • you have little to no previous French instruction
  • you have some French but want to build a stronger foundation before moving to intermediate-level programs

Who Should Attend

This course is designed for anyone starting or restarting their French, including:

  • Complete beginners with no prior French instruction
  • Elementary learners looking to solidify basics before the TCF/TEF Pathway
  • Anyone planning to eventually work toward the TCF Canada or TEF Canada
