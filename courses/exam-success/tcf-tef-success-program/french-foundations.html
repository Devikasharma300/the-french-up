About This Course

French becomes easier when the basics start connecting.

French Foundations is designed for learners who are new to French or who have studied a little but still feel unsure about how sentences are built.

The course gives you a working base in essential grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation and everyday communication. Instead of trying to memorize everything at once, you learn the patterns that help you build and understand more French over time.

How Foundations Progresses

Step 1

Understand the building blocks

Learn the sentence patterns, core vocabulary and grammar that make basic communication possible.

Step 2

Use them in real situations

Ask questions, respond, describe, listen and write through familiar everyday contexts.

Step 3

Rely less on models

Begin creating more of your own sentences and responses instead of depending on fixed examples.

What You'll Learn

The focus is on the French you need to start communicating, not on memorizing isolated rules.

Build useful sentences

Learn how French sentences work so you can move beyond isolated words and express complete ideas.

Talk about everyday situations

Introduce yourself, ask and answer questions, describe people and routines, and talk about plans, preferences and familiar situations.

Understand essential grammar

Work with core verb forms, articles, gender, agreement, questions, negation and sentence structure.

Build vocabulary you can reuse

Develop high-frequency vocabulary and learn how to apply it across different situations.

Start understanding spoken French

Learn to recognize familiar words, phrases and patterns in slow and clearly structured French.

Learn pronunciation from the beginning

Work on common French sounds, letter combinations and basic rhythm while you are building vocabulary.

Who Should Attend

French Foundations is for learners who need a clear starting point and enough time to make the basics usable before moving into more demanding work.

This course may be right for you if...

  • you're completely new to French
  • you've studied some French before but still struggle to build simple sentences
  • grammar feels disconnected and difficult to apply
  • you recognize some words but cannot yet follow or produce much connected French
  • you want to work toward TCF or TEF later, but need the language base first

You may be ready for the Pathway if...

  • you can already manage simple everyday conversations in French
  • you understand basic sentence structure and common verb forms
  • your main challenge is making your French longer, more accurate and more flexible
  • you are ready to work with more natural listening, longer reading and more developed responses
You do not need to identify your level perfectly. If you are unsure whether to begin with French Foundations or the TCF/TEF Pathway, contact us and we can help you choose.
