Why a pathway?

Exam strategy cannot replace French proficiency.

Some learners arrive ready to practise exam technique. Others need more time to build the language itself.

Instead of placing every learner into the same preparation course, we help you start at the stage that matches your current ability.

This creates a more realistic, supportive and effective route toward TCF or TEF preparation.

Learn before you test

Beginners focus first on building the French they will eventually need for higher-level communication.

Introduce exam tasks gradually

Pathway learners begin working with structured speaking, listening, reading and writing activities without turning every class into a mock exam.

Prepare differently when you are ready

Exam-ready learners work more intensively on timing, task strategy, simulations, corrections and performance.

TEF vs. TCF

Which exam is right for you?

Both the TEF Canada and the TCF Canada are accepted by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) for economic immigration and citizenship applications. They assess the same four skills, but the way they're structured — and which one fits you best — can differ.

  TEF Canada TCF Canada
Full name Test d'évaluation de français – Canada Test de connaissance du français – Canada
Administered by Chambre de Commerce et d'Industrie de Paris Île-de-France (CCIP) France Éducation international
Accepted by IRCC Yes Yes
Skills assessed Listening, reading, writing, speaking Listening, reading, writing, speaking
Speaking format Two structured role-plays (e.g. a phone call and a persuasion task) A more open, interview-style conversation
Task style Generally follows a fixed, repeatable structure Tasks can vary more from session to session
Results valid for 2 years 2 years
Availability Longer-established, offered at more test centres worldwide Fewer centres; sessions can be less frequent in some regions

Important: Choosing the Right French Exam

We can help you assess your current French level, understand the TCF and TEF formats, develop your language skills, and prepare for exam tasks and strategies.

However, we do not determine which exam you should take or which score, NCLC level, or program requirement applies to your specific immigration, citizenship, professional, or personal situation.

Before registering for an exam or preparation program, please confirm the current requirements with the relevant government authority, institution, regulatory body, or other official organization responsible for your application.
