About This Course

Specialized preparation for the Government of Canada Second Language Evaluation. This program focuses on the skills required to succeed in the Oral Language Assessment.

What You Will Learn

  • Workplace vocabulary
  • Oral interview practice
  • Expanding answers
  • Grammar accuracy
  • Mock evaluations
  • Level C communication strategies

Prerequisites

Students should already have conversational French.

Recommended: B1–B2 level.

Who Should Attend

  • Government employees
  • Federal job applicants
  • Professionals preparing for SLE testing
