Why a pathway?

Exam strategy cannot replace French proficiency.

Some learners arrive ready to practise exam technique. Others need more time to build the language itself.

Instead of placing every learner into the same preparation course, we help you start at the stage that matches your current ability.

This creates a more realistic, supportive and effective route toward TCF or TEF preparation.

Learn before you test

Beginners focus first on building the French they will eventually need for higher-level communication.

Introduce exam tasks gradually

Pathway learners begin working with structured speaking, listening, reading and writing activities without turning every class into a mock exam.

Prepare differently when you are ready

Exam-ready learners work more intensively on timing, task strategy, simulations, corrections and performance.

Build the French you need before exam preparation begins.

French Foundations is designed for complete beginners and elementary learners who need to build a solid base before progressing toward TCF or TEF-focused training.

The program includes internal learning levels, but you do not need to choose one yourself. We assess your current French and place you in the appropriate group.

Speaking

Develop confidence forming sentences, answering questions and speaking about everyday situations.

Listening

Build your ability to understand common words, phrases, questions and increasingly natural spoken French.

Reading

Understand short messages, everyday texts and increasingly detailed written information.

Writing

Learn to construct clear sentences and communicate basic information accurately in writing.

TCF / TEF Pathway

Elementary to developing intermediate learners

$595

10 weeks · 30 instructional hours

  • Four-skill language development
  • Guided speaking practice
  • Structured writing feedback
  • Gradual exam familiarization

Strengthen your French while beginning to think like an exam candidate.

The TCF / TEF Pathway is for learners who already have basic French but are not yet ready for an intensive exam preparation course.

The priority remains language development. At the same time, you begin working with more structured communication tasks, timed activities and the types of skills that later become important in TCF or TEF preparation.

Speaking

Give longer answers, explain experiences, compare options, express opinions and support your ideas.

Listening

Understand increasingly natural French and identify key information more efficiently.

Reading

Work with longer everyday, professional and opinion-based texts while improving comprehension speed.

Writing

Organize ideas more clearly, describe events and develop increasingly detailed written responses.
