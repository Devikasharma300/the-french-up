About This Course

Introduction

This course is designed to help you prepare for the Government of Canada Second Language Evaluation (SLE) Oral Test at Level C. Through advanced speaking practice, you will strengthen your ability to discuss complex workplace topics, express and defend nuanced opinions, and communicate with greater fluency, precision, and confidence in preparation for the Oral Language Assessment (OLA).

What You'll Learn

1. Discuss Complex Workplace Issues

  • Discuss leadership styles, workplace challenges, and decision-making
  • Explore organizational change and its impact on employees and teams
  • Explain professional situations in detail and reflect on their broader implications

2. Express, Develop, and Defend Complex Opinions

  • Clearly express nuanced points of view on workplace and social issues
  • Develop your ideas using relevant reasons, examples, and supporting arguments
  • Compare different perspectives, respond to challenging questions, and defend your position
  • Adapt your response as a discussion develops

3. Discuss Workplace Well-Being and Team Dynamics

  • Explore topics related to motivation, engagement, and workplace culture
  • Discuss stress, burnout, work-life balance, and employee well-being
  • Analyze factors that contribute to effective teamwork and collaboration

4. Communicate with Greater Fluency and Precision

  • Use complex sentence structures to communicate detailed and interconnected ideas
  • Discuss hypothetical situations, conditions, consequences, and possible outcomes
  • Use a broader range of vocabulary and expressions appropriate to professional discussions
  • Apply advanced grammar, including the subjunctive, pronouns, and past tenses, with greater accuracy
  • Organize your ideas clearly during longer and more complex responses

Important Note on Learning Pace

Everyone learns at a different pace. Progress may vary depending on your previous experience, current language level, learning style, and the amount of practice you are able to do outside of class.

Some learners may need additional time, review, or practice to fully develop the advanced speaking skills covered in this course.

The learning outcomes describe the skills you will work toward during the course, but achieving Level C may require additional practice, continued exposure to French, and regular use of the language in real-life and professional situations.

Prerequisites

This is an Advanced course (CEFR B2).You should enroll in this course if:

  • you have completed French at the B1 level
  • you have an advanced level of French

Who Should Attend

This course is for anyone looking to improve their language skills for professional reasons. This includes:

  • Government employees seeking to enhance their French language proficiency ahead of the Public Service Commission SLE (Second Language Evaluation) to reach level B
  • Anyone who meets the prerequisites
