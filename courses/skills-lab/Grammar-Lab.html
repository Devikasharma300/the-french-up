Why Grammar Lab?

Knowing the rule is one thing. Using it is another.

The subjunctive, ne explétif, confusing agreements, tricky prepositions, pronouns y and en, dont versus que, passé composé versus imparfait—and those exceptions that never seem to stick. We focus on the parts that trip you up when you actually need them.

Bring a sentence from a book, a confusing correction or a question that a tutorial never quite settled. It might become a future Lab topic.

Workshop topic 01 · The subjunctive
Je pense qu'il vient.
Je ne pense pas qu'il vienne.

Why does the verb change?

Explore how meaning and sentence structure affect the mood.

Workshop topic 02 · Ne explétif

Why is there a ne without a negative meaning?

Make sense of a little word that can be surprisingly confusing.

Workshop topic 03 · Hypothetical situations

Why these two verb forms together?

Practise expressing unreal situations in the past.

Workshop topic 04 · Past participle agreement

Why isn't lavé feminine here?

See what changes the agreement in this construction.
