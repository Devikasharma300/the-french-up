About This Course

Build a strong foundation in French and develop the confidence to handle everyday conversations.

Designed for beginners and learners returning to French, this program offers a structured pathway from essential grammar and vocabulary to practical communication. You'll work on all four language skills—listening, speaking, reading, and writing—in an encouraging environment.

Your learning journey

Explore foundational A1 topics and begin working toward A2-level language skills through explanations, guided practice, and interactive tasks.

A note about progress

Everyone learns at a different pace. The 24-hour group course introduces and develops skills associated with A1–A2, but reaching full A2 proficiency may require additional study and independent practice.

Understanding French levels

What do A1 and A2 actually mean?

A1 and A2 are the two beginner levels of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), a widely used guide to language ability. In simple terms, A1 means you can use basic words and phrases for everyday situations, while A2 means you can handle simple, routine conversations about familiar topics with more confidence.

A1 French Level (Beginner)

At this level, learners are generally able to:

  • use common words and simple expressions
  • introduce themselves and others
  • ask and answer basic questions about topics such as home, family, hobbies, or daily life
  • communicate in simple situations when others speak slowly and clearly

A2 French Level (Elementary)

At this level, learners are generally able to:

  • understand common expressions related to everyday life
  • talk about topics such as family, shopping, work, or local surroundings
  • exchange simple information in everyday situations
  • describe personal experiences and basic needs in simple terms
