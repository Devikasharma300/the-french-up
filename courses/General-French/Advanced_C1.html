About This Course

Advanced French coaching for learners who need greater precision, confidence and professional-level communication.

French Advanced helps you refine your ability to understand complex French, express nuanced ideas, and adapt your language to different audiences and contexts. Work with authentic materials, advanced discussions, practical writing and focused instructor feedback.

Important note on learning pace

Everyone learns at a different pace. Progress may vary depending on your previous experience, current language level, learning style, and the amount of practice you are able to do outside of class.

Some learners may need additional time, review, or practice to fully develop the skills covered in this course.

The learning outcomes describe the skills you will work toward during the course, but reaching C1 may require additional practice and continued use of French in real-life situations.

Course curriculum

What You'll Learn

Build precise and flexible communication for demanding professional, academic and social contexts.

Advanced Speaking

  • Present ideas with clarity and structure
  • Debate abstract and complex issues

Grammar & Expression

Professional Communication

  • Lead and contribute to meetings

These topics are illustrative examples, not an exhaustive list.

Reading, listening, writing, pronunciation and speaking are integrated throughout. Activities are tailored to the group's starting abilities.

For accomplished French learners

Who Should Join

  • Advanced French learners seeking higher precision and confidence

Recommended starting point

Prerequisites

A B2 level or equivalent is recommended.

You should consider this course if you:

  • Have completed French Intermediate (B1–B2) or have equivalent French proficiency.
  • Can communicate comfortably in French in everyday, social, and professional situations.
  • Can express opinions clearly and support them with reasons and examples.
  • Can understand extended conversations, discussions, and more complex written texts.
  • Have a good command of French grammar but want to improve accuracy, fluency, and nuanced expression.
  • Are ready to engage with advanced topics, debates, and more sophisticated language.
You do not need to identify your own CEFR level perfectly. If you are unsure whether you belong in the French Advanced (C1) program, contact us and we can help confirm the best starting point.

Understanding French levels

What does C1 French mean?

C1 is an advanced proficiency level in the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). It means you can understand demanding material, express complex ideas with relatively little hesitation, and use French flexibly for social, academic and professional purposes.

C1 · ADVANCED

Skilled, flexible communicator

At this level, learners are able to:

  • Follow long and complex texts, talks and discussions
  • Present ideas clearly with a logical structure
  • Use French effectively in professional or academic settings
  • Express ideas with nuance and appropriate tone
