Cancellation, Withdrawal and Refund Policy

Definitions

For the purposes of this policy:

  • “Business Day” means Monday through Friday, excluding statutory holidays observed in Ontario.
  • “Business Hours” means 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a Business Day.

For the purposes of The French Up's contractual cancellation deadlines, written notices received after 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, or on a day that is not a Business Day, will be treated as received on the next Business Day. This provision does not limit any right of cancellation or notice period provided by applicable law.

Student Cancellation or Withdrawal

Students may cancel their registration by providing written notice to The French Up at least 15 Business Days prior to the scheduled start date of the course or session.

Where cancellation is received at least 15 Business Days prior to the scheduled start date, all course fees paid will be refunded, less any non-refundable registration or administrative fee that was clearly disclosed to the student before registration and permitted by applicable law.

For cancellations received fewer than 15 Business Days prior to the scheduled start date, or after the course has commenced, course fees are non-refundable except where otherwise required by applicable law.

All cancellation and withdrawal requests must be submitted in writing. The effective date of cancellation will be the date on which The French Up receives the written notice, subject to the Business Hours provision above and any applicable statutory rules governing notice.

Course Transfers and Substitutions

At The French Up's discretion, a student may request to transfer their registration to another available session instead of cancelling their registration.

Transfer requests must be made before the start of the registered session and are subject to:

  • course availability
  • appropriate language-level placement
  • any applicable course prerequisites

Approval of a transfer is not guaranteed.

Minimum Enrolment and Cancellation by The French Up

Group courses require a minimum enrolment of four students in order to commence.

The French Up reserves the right to cancel or postpone a group course before its scheduled start date where the minimum enrolment has not been reached or where circumstances reasonably prevent the course from proceeding as scheduled.

If The French Up cancels a group course before it begins because minimum enrolment has not been reached, the student may choose either:

  • a full refund of all amounts paid for the cancelled course, including any registration or administrative fees associated with that course; or
  • transfer to a future group session of the same or a comparable course, subject to availability and the student's agreement; or
  • transfer to private instruction, subject to availability and the student's agreement to pay any applicable difference between the group-course fee and the private-instruction fee.

A student will never be mandatorily required to transfer to another group session or to private instruction.

If the student does not choose one of the transfer options, The French Up will issue a full refund.

Once a group course has commenced, a subsequent reduction in enrolment below four students will not result in cancellation of the course solely because enrolment has fallen below the minimum.

Students who remain enrolled will continue to receive the course for which they registered. They will not be required to pay an additional fee because other students have withdrawn.

Changes to Course Schedule, Instructor or Virtual Delivery

The French Up may make reasonable administrative changes to instructors, virtual platforms, or other non-material course arrangements where necessary.

A change of instructor or virtual platform will not, by itself, constitute cancellation of the course or create an entitlement to a refund, provided that the student continues to receive substantially the same course and amount of instruction for which they registered.

The French Up will not materially reduce the number of instructional hours purchased by a student without the student's agreement.

Where The French Up proposes a material change to the scheduled dates or times that prevents a student from reasonably participating, the student will be given the option to:

  • transfer to another suitable available session; or
  • receive a refund for any affected instruction that cannot be provided.

If the course has not yet commenced and the material change prevents the student from participating in the course, the student may request a full refund.

No student will be required to accept a materially different course, schedule, or level in place of the course for which they registered.

Missed Classes and Student Absences

Students are responsible for attending their scheduled classes.

Except where otherwise required by applicable law, no refund, replacement class, or reduction in fees will be provided for classes missed because of a student's absence, late arrival, scheduling conflict, illness, travel, employment obligation, personal technical difficulties, or other personal circumstances.

Virtual Class Access and Technical Requirements

The French Up currently provides instruction virtually.

Students are responsible for having:

  • a reliable internet connection
  • a compatible computer, tablet, or other suitable device
  • functioning audio and, where required, video capabilities
  • any software or virtual platform reasonably required to participate in the course

The French Up is not responsible for missed or interrupted instruction caused by a student's internet connection, equipment, software, device, or other technical problem outside The French Up's reasonable control.

Private Lessons

A student may cancel their registration for private instruction by providing written notice to The French Up at least 15 Business Days prior to the scheduled start date of the private course, package, or instruction period.

Where cancellation is received at least 15 Business Days before the scheduled start date, fees paid will be refunded in accordance with the Student Cancellation or Withdrawal section of this policy.

Where cancellation occurs fewer than 15 Business Days before the scheduled start date, or after the private instruction has commenced, fees are non-refundable except where otherwise required by applicable law.

Requests to reschedule individual private lessons after private instruction has commenced are subject to the scheduling terms disclosed to the student at the time of registration or booking.

The French Up-Initiated Class Cancellation

If The French Up cancels an individual class, The French Up will make reasonable efforts to reschedule the class or provide an equivalent replacement class at no additional charge.

If The French Up cannot provide the cancelled class or an equivalent replacement class, the student will receive a refund for the portion of instruction that was not provided.

Where repeated cancellations or schedule changes by The French Up materially affect the course as a whole, any additional cancellation or refund rights available under applicable law will continue to apply.

Force Majeure and Circumstances Beyond The French Up's Control

The French Up will not be liable for delays or interruptions caused by circumstances beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, severe weather affecting instructors or essential infrastructure, public health emergencies, government orders, labour disruptions, widespread power or internet outages, major technology or virtual-platform failures, or similar events.

Where reasonably possible under these circumstances, The French Up will make reasonable efforts to reschedule affected instruction or provide an equivalent alternative method of virtual instruction.

Students will not be charged additional fees solely because instruction must be rescheduled as a result of such circumstances.

Refund Processing

Refunds will be issued to the original payer and, where reasonably possible, through the original method of payment.

Refunds approved under The French Up's voluntary cancellation policy will be processed within 15 Business Days after approval.

Where applicable law requires a refund to be provided within a shorter period, the legally required timeframe will apply.

Where a student exercises a statutory right to cancel an agreement or receive a refund, The French Up will provide all refunds required by applicable law within the applicable statutory timeframe.

No provision of this policy requiring payment to be non-refundable will apply where applicable law requires The French Up to provide a refund.

Promotional, discounted, bundled, or specially priced registrations remain subject to this policy unless different terms were clearly disclosed to the student before registration and those terms comply with applicable law.

Online Registrations and Statutory Consumer Rights

Where a registration is completed online and is subject to Ontario's consumer protection legislation, The French Up will provide the information and documentation required by applicable law.

Where required, students will be provided with a clear opportunity to review the applicable course information, pricing, fees, terms, cancellation policy, and other required information before completing their registration.

Students will also be given an opportunity to correct errors and to expressly accept or decline the agreement before completing the transaction where required by law.

The French Up will provide students with a copy or confirmation of the applicable agreement in a form that the student can retain and access for future reference where required by law.

Any statutory cancellation or refund right arising from an online or other consumer agreement applies regardless of any different deadline or restriction contained elsewhere in this policy.

Applicable Law and Statutory Rights

This policy and registrations with The French Up are governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the applicable federal laws of Canada.

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts, waives, or is intended to waive any right, remedy, guarantee, or protection that cannot lawfully be excluded or waived under applicable legislation.

Where this policy provides a student with greater cancellation or refund rights than the minimum rights required by law, the more favourable provision of this policy will apply.

Where applicable law provides the student with greater rights than this policy, the applicable law will prevail.

If any provision of this policy is determined to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, that provision will be interpreted or limited to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions will continue in effect to the extent permitted by law.

Policy Changes

The French Up may amend this policy from time to time to reflect changes to its operations or applicable law.

Except where a change is required by law, the version of this policy in effect on the date a student completes their registration will apply to that registration.

A later amendment will not retroactively reduce a student's contractual or statutory rights.
